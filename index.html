Screenless carry

The idea

Take your phone.
Choose your screen.

Your phone number, apps, and files live on a pocket-sized core. The screen clips on when you need it. At the desk, dock into a mini tablet and pick up the draft you started on the way home.

Slip on a handheld screen for everyday use. Clip the core to your bag and take calls on earbuds. A foldable display could give the same phone more room when it opens.

Inside Puck

Everything that makes it yours.

Puck is designed around a single home for your compute, storage, battery, and cellular connection. Displays and mounts attach around it.

The core
One place for your phone number, apps, files, and ongoing tasks.
The connection
A mechanical latch takes the load. The prototype will test wired video, touch, and power.
The control
A physical button and earbuds make calls and voice notes possible with the screen detached.

Development roadmap

From concept to carry.

The first build has one job. Make a task feel continuous as the screen changes.

  1. Current stage

    Concept

    Define the core, the attachments, and a screenless interaction worth carrying.

  2. Prototype

    Build a working Android donor rig and move one live workflow between screen sizes.

  3. Validation

    Test attachment wear, touch, charging, secure unlock, and calls across screen swaps.

  4. Production planning

    Confirm components, carrier compatibility, costs, and delivery estimates before taking pledges.

FAQ

Good questions.

What can I do with the screen off?

The proposed screenless mode pairs a physical button with Bluetooth earbuds for calls and voice notes. The prototype will test those actions while the phone is locked and the screen is detached.

What happens to my apps when I swap screens?

Your apps and files stay on the core. The first prototype will test reopening the same draft on a different screen. Handoffs for other apps will depend on how they handle display changes.

Will the foldable screen be in the first kit?

The first kits focus on a clip, a handheld screen, and a mini-tablet screen. The foldable is a concept for a later attachment.

Will Puck work with my carrier?

Carrier and region support will depend on the radio hardware, testing, and certification. Compatibility details are still open.

Can I place an order today?

Puck is at the concept stage. The renders and rewards show what we are exploring. You can preview a tier here. Pledges and payments open only if the project launches a live campaign.
